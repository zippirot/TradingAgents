## Engine evidence map and fixture guidance

`financial_evidence.facts` retains values, periods, metrics, units and source IDs. `financial_evidence.sources` retains SEC accessions, CIK, filing dates, URLs and precise XBRL locators. These are normalized source records. Resolve IDs and check issuer/date/definition before comparing a claim.

`filing_evidence.documents` retains SEC filing metadata, source IDs, URLs, content hashes, retrieval dates and individual blocks with locators, text, currency and truncation flags. The current statements contain comparable raw table passages. Documents have selected-section limits, so absence from these blocks does not prove absence from the original filing.

`disclosure_evidence.kpi_comparisons[].sources` and `.source_packages` include RPO exact inline tags, unit tags, context tags and character offsets. These permit an independent recomputation from explicitly scoped inputs. In this archive, the RPO originals are richer than the shared brief supplied to analysts.

`valuation_reference.raw_sources.history` retains dated completed observations, adjustment labels and source hashes. `.own_history.rows` retains price, shares, revenue and source lineage. `.subject` supplies the current corresponding basis. The market-tool records retain only bounded excerpts and disclose truncation. Their presence does not establish unexcerpted price history or exchange-calendar completeness.
