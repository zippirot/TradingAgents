## Outcome and scope

When a large US domestic filer's statements contain the required evidence and the analyst's material claims validate, the report should reach the research debate and the Portfolio Manager's decision. When a required item cannot be verified, it should stop at REVIEW and tell the reader what was missing, for which period, and where the app looked. The main test is whether more *previously unseen* companies clear the seven source checks without introducing incorrect passes. Live confirmation tests whether the app actually delivers the decision.

Earlier Microsoft runs stopped before debate (latest publication `924c8faf` on `55edfd7e`). Four checks were unavailable: obligations, equity movements, cash-flow coverage and disclosure coverage. Caterpillar's earlier proof also exposed differences in statement layout and accounting labels. These are development examples, not evidence that all large issuers have the same problem. In particular, the earlier estimate of a roughly 24 MB Microsoft submission does not establish the size of the primary HTML document or which reader limit blocked it.

Keep every existing safety check. Do not introduce a “not applicable” shortcut, convert a missing fact to zero, or count a narrower total as a complete one. Banks, insurers, REITs and other excluded financials, foreign filers using 20-F or 40-F, and issuers with multiple common share classes are outside this build. The rating remains a research opinion; the build does not size positions or place orders.

## Freeze the companies before inspecting evidence

1. Use a dated copy of SEC's `company_tickers.json` in published order. Select the first **30 distinct eligible issuer IDs (CIKs)**, keeping the first eligible occurrence of an issuer. Describe the cohort as “first eligible issuers in the frozen SEC order.” The [SEC describes the file](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) as a ticker/CIK association, not a market-cap ranking.
2. Before running selection, specify and freeze the dated eligibility metadata and the fields used to establish US domestic 10-K **and** 10-Q filing status, SIC outside 6000–6799, and exactly one outstanding common share class, including unlisted classes. A single listed ticker does not prove a single class. Screen mechanically from metadata without opening candidate filings, facts or check results. SEC's ticker and submissions files do not list share classes, so the permitted class source is the **cover-page facts of each candidate's latest eligible 10-K or 10-Q filed by the frozen cutoff** (the `dei` registered-security and shares-outstanding entries, including any class dimensions), read by script for eligibility only, since a newer quarterly cover can disclose a class change. Nothing else in those filings may be read or printed during selection. The script records the accession, measurement date, eligibility decision and its basis; ambiguous cases are skipped. If a field is unknown or ambiguous, skip the issuer and record why. If a reproducible source cannot establish eligibility for 30 issuers, report that limitation; do not substitute a proxy.
3. Put MSFT, CAT and WMT in the scored development group **only if in the 30**. Fill the remaining development slots in ascending SHA-256 order of the UTF-8 string `large-filer-2026-11-09:` plus the frozen uppercase ticker (CIK breaks a tie), until development has exactly **10**. The other **20** are held out. A named anchor outside the 30 is an extra development example outside both denominators. If MSFT falls outside the 30, capture it and run L0 on it at baseline and final as a control, so its paid run has an offline comparison. DOCN is a separate regression control. Neither extras nor DOCN enter either denominator.
4. Record the order, metadata sources, retrieval dates, hashes, eligibility decisions, issuer list and split. Verify 30 distinct CIKs, disjoint groups of 10 and 20, and no control in either group. Freeze the analysis cutoff, scoring definition, draw procedures and selection manifest before either score is seen. No issuer replacements or group changes after the freeze.

During development, inspect only development examples and DOCN. Automated raw capture and the baseline and final scoring runs may process held-out data; the builder may not open, search or print its filings, facts, check results, traces or intermediate summaries. Seal the held-out baseline details and expose only a completion receipt. A leak must be recorded and invalidates the independent verdict for this cohort; it is not repaired by moving the issuer to development.

## Build sequence

### L0. Build and freeze the offline scorer first

Use a script in a new `scripts/` directory and focused tests. Drive the real first graph step, `tradingagents/agents/evidence_validation.py::create_source_acquisition(["fundamentals"])`, and read the statuses it stores in `evidence_acquisition.source_checks`. That step already runs SEC fact and filing retrieval, document preparation and `assess_financial_package(..., source_only=True)` in production order. Do not implement a second version of that sequence or of the accounting checks, or start the analyst graph. Replay raw SEC responses beneath `requests.get`, so transport limits, parsing and package selection all execute; non-SEC sources are off and cannot affect the seven checks. First run this step once against live SEC for MSFT, at no model cost, and verify that replaying the capture reproduces the same statuses and reasons. If it cannot run faithfully without analysts, stop before L1.

- **Capture once, score twice.** Pin issuer, cutoff, raw company facts, submissions metadata, primary filing documents, relevant amendments/exhibits and their hashes. Capture every document listed in the filing index of every 10-K, 10-K/A, 10-Q, 10-Q/A, 8-K and 8-K/A from the latest eligible 10-K through the cutoff, including documents the baseline reader may omit, because the final code will request documents the baseline never did. Preserve accession, URL and source vintage. Both revisions must independently build their own bounded packages, search corpus and verified cells from those same raw inputs. Replaying the old reader's selected documents or extracted cells would conceal the changes being tested.
- **Score with network and model access disabled.** The existing `tradingagents/source_replay.py` can call a live provider on a replay miss. In the scorer, a miss or hash mismatch is an input failure; do not fetch a substitute or describe it as an omission by the filer. Keep capture/replay failures visible in the result. One exception is predeclared, for a miss on a document that belongs to an accession in the frozen capture scope and was filed by the cutoff: before anything is unsealed, an automated step fetches that document by accession, records its hash in the manifest without displaying its content, and rescores that issuer on both revisions with the production code unchanged. Report the number of such fills per group. A miss outside that scope, or a fill that fails, remains an input failure.
- **Record live timing, because replay cannot show it.** The reader's 90-second budget cannot trigger without network delay, so a company can pass offline and time out live. During capture, record each company's elapsed time, request count and any exhausted budget on the baseline code. After the final score, run the same free SEC-only pass on the frozen candidate. Held-out companies expose only those three values. Report any company that reaches 7/7 offline but exhausts a budget live; it is a live-delivery risk beside the offline score, and the score itself stays unchanged.
- **Score exactly seven codes:** `source_integrity`, `core_statements`, `balance_sheet`, `equity_movement`, `obligation_perimeter`, `cash_flow_perimeter`, `disclosure_coverage`. A company earns **7/7 source checks** only when every code explicitly passes. An omitted required check is unavailable; optional checks cannot substitute. Preserve each status and reason. Never label 7/7 as live “sufficient”: the separate, required `material_claims` check depends on the analyst report, and actual routing is assessed later.
- **Freeze the measurement.** Pin the scorer, input manifest and scoring rules; the production code under test is the intended variable. Score all 30 plus DOCN (and MSFT as a control if it is outside the 30) on the starting production revision **before L1–L5**. Record the baseline code and scorer revisions. Keep development and control results available; seal held-out results as described above.

### L1. Establish the scope of obligations at the balance-sheet date

Use `dataflows/filing_facts.py`, `agents/evidence_validation.py::obligation_checks` and `agents/accounting_checks.py::calculate_obligations` under `tradingagents/`. Trace the resulting categories into valuation and reporting, including `agents/valuation_cases.py`. For each fact or scope determination, retain the exact dated row or sentence, filing accession, locator and hash.

- Accept a numeric zero only for the exact instrument or category the filing establishes: a zero or zero-indicating dash in the correct row, column, unit and date; an explicit statement of absence; or a dated, explicitly exhaustive inventory that unambiguously excludes that category. “No commercial paper outstanding” does not prove all short-term borrowings are zero. A blank, missing tag, ambiguous dash or incomplete note proves nothing.
- Equipment financing can be reported as **not presented** when complete, untruncated debt and lease disclosures support that conclusion. Record which categories are covered and how they avoid overlap. This is a nonnumeric description. If only a narrower total is proved, label that total accurately and leave the broader required total unavailable in the gate, valuation and report. Preserve the existing checks for lease obligations, unrestricted cash, and conflicts between a combined total and separately reported amounts.
- **Make a proved zero citable.** `valuation_cases.py` (`_borrowings_complete` in `valuation.py`) accepts a borrowings perimeter only from a sourced Total Borrowings fact or complete short-term plus long-term component facts. When L1 establishes a zero short-term balance by explicit statement or exhaustive inventory, emit it as a sourced zero fact (metric "Short-term Borrowings", the sentence or row as locator) so the gate, the valuation case and the report use the same evidence. Without this, Microsoft would clear the gate and still have no usable valuation case.

### L2. Reconcile equity using the actual owners and period

Read the stockholders' equity statement table and map movements by their accounting meaning, including dividends, repurchases, issuance, stock-based compensation and other comprehensive income. Update the filing-cell verifier, SEC fact mapping and roll-forward calculation together. The reviewed mapper (`dataflows/vendors/sec_edgar.py`) groups parent and consolidated equity under one metric; the table parser (`dataflows/filing_facts.py`) expects one total column.

Carry the ownership basis, source column and actual period through the opening balance, every signed movement and the closing balance. Match to the corresponding parent or consolidated balance on the face statement; use income on that same basis. Establish the opening and closing interval from filing dates and column headers so quarter and year-to-date figures cannot be crossed. If a movement or ownership basis is ambiguous, leave the check unavailable and identify the row or column. A table adding up does not establish whose equity it represents.

### L3. Establish principal payments over the cash-flow period

In `agents/evidence_validation.py::cash_flow_checks` and its calculation tests, retain the production distinction between a closing balance and cash paid over a period. Require operating cash flow, cash capex and the existing spending classifications, plus either a verified combined principal amount or complete, source-linked evidence for the lessee's principal payments over the same cash-flow period.

A lease-principal-only result can satisfy the required perimeter **only when independent same-period evidence establishes complete coverage** (for example, explicit zero equipment-principal payments or a complete, non-overlapping classification of principal paid). L1's period-end “not presented” status cannot supply that proof. An issuer may pay off its equipment debt during the quarter and finish with no liability. Keep lessor revenue, leased-to-others assets, equipment acquisition and financing proceeds distinct from lessee principal. If full coverage is not proved, retain REVIEW.

### L4. Retrieve the relevant filing sections and account for omissions

Work in `dataflows/vendors/sec_documents.py`, the accounting-cell verifier and `agents/evidence_validation.py::disclosure_coverage_check`. Diagnose three different quantities on development data: size of the full SEC submission, bytes of the primary document actually requested, and characters retained in excerpts. Both reviewed readers impose a **16,000,000-byte transport ceiling**; the **120,000-character** package budget applies after retrieval. Full-document search has separate filing and document limits. Also measure the caps in `get_filing_evidence` and `select_filings`: **6 selected filings, 8 documents, and 10 requests within 90 seconds** per package. Each can produce a gap outside the informational list and so stop `disclosure_coverage`. The text budget is also consumed quickly: at the per-document ceilings, 10-K 40,000 + 10-Q 40,000 + earnings 8-K 4,000 + its release 36,000 uses all 120,000, after which every further filing is a blocking omission. Adjust only the demonstrated bottlenecks, with bounded retrieval and extraction, and report the document-count gap only when a document was actually left out (the reviewed document-count check in `sec_documents.py`, beside the 8-document cap, also fires at exactly 8 documents with nothing remaining; line numbers moved after `0236255d`, so locate it by the cap).

**Separate coverage from the model excerpt.** Keep the 120,000-character package that agents read, as the defect-closure build's task S2 does (its S1 is the audit budget). A filing that was retrieved, parsed and accounted for must not become a blocking omission merely because its text did not fit that excerpt; the excerpt limit stays recorded as informational and says so. Do not raise the 120,000 figure unless measurement shows no other way to meet the outcome, and if so, report the effect on prompt size and cost first. The accounting checks read the bounded filing package in both the pre-analyst and final assessments, so a document reachable only through later search does not help them. If a limit rises, record MSFT's package size and acquisition time before and after.

Prioritize the face statements, equity table, debt and lease notes, then subsequent event filings and relevant exhibits; narrative comes last. Reuse retained sources with matching issuer, accession, date and content hash rather than fetching twice. Make complete relevant tables and source locators available to the accounting verifier. The presence of a search corpus or an excerpt does not prove completeness.

Enumerate **all** subsequent 8-Ks and 8-K/As through the cutoff from submissions metadata **before** filtering by item code. Account for each omission with accession, form/item, omission stage and reason. Preserve the primary filing, relevant amendments and material exhibits. A missing or unclassified item that might change a check remains blocking, including financing disclosures and [Item 4.02 non-reliance notices](https://www.sec.gov/answers/form8k.htm). Mark an omission informational only when its lack of impact is established. The reviewed bounded selector silently omitted a synthetic 4.02 event before it reached the excerpt budget.

**A retrieved non-reliance notice must affect the verdict, not just be retained.** The reviewed `disclosure_coverage_check` returned `pass` on a synthetic quarterly filing with a successfully retrieved notice withdrawing reliance on that quarter. When an Item 4.02 notice covers a period or statement used as an input to the assessment, and the frozen evidence contains no verified correction (a restated filing for that period), `disclosure_coverage` must not pass and the run stops at REVIEW, naming the notice. A notice covering only an unrelated period must not block a valid current report. If the affected periods cannot be established, keep it blocking.

### L5. Give the reader the actual REVIEW reason

For every blocking check, retain the missing or conflicting item, date or period, sources searched, accession/locator when available, and reason the check did not pass. Render all blockers in the full REVIEW report even if the executive summary is abbreviated. Distinguish “could not retrieve the filing,” “table was truncated or ambiguous,” and “complete filing evidence does not state the item.” Do not tell the reader the filer omitted something when the reader itself failed to reach the source.

## Acceptance

### 1. Verify and freeze the candidate before opening held-out results

Exercise transport failures and excerpt truncation separately. Verify the report distinguishes unavailable sources from actual nondisclosure.

Run the full default `pytest -q` and `ruff check`; record results, counts and skips.

**Real-path proof before the freeze (only with paid-run approval).** Run MSFT, a development example, once through the real app at Medium depth: `.venv/bin/python -m cli.main` from the feature worktree on Machine 2, with `TRADINGAGENTS_RESULTS_DIR`, `TRADINGAGENTS_CACHE_DIR` and `TRADINGAGENTS_MEMORY_LOG_PATH` pointing to a disposable directory. Inspect the saved report and full-state log for the seven source checks, `material_claims`, the route taken, and either the Portfolio Manager decision or a true REVIEW reason. The final assessment also reads the analysts' document searches, so `source_integrity` can differ from the offline score. Fix any blocker here, while no held-out result has been opened. If paid approval has not been given, continue and record that this proof is missing.

Commit the candidate, freeze its exact revision and confirm the scorer, inputs, cohort and draw procedures match the baseline manifest. Run the final L0 score once on all 30 plus DOCN (and the MSFT control, if applicable) with the same inputs and keep the candidate fixed. Only then unseal the held-out baseline and final results.

### 2. Apply the predeclared held-out verdict

For each group, **gain = (number at 7/7 after − number at 7/7 before) / original group size**. The denominators are always 10 and 20. Let D and H be the respective gains in percentage points. The numerical criterion is **at least 5 of the 20 held-out companies newly reach 7/7 (H ≥ 25 percentage points)**. The difference between D and H does not decide the result. Report it prominently and state plainly when development improved far more than held-out. The floor is a practical minimum, not a statistical confidence bound. State results as counts (for example, "six additional held-out companies cleared all seven checks"). The earlier comparative rule was dropped because, with groups this small, it failed 21–26% of the time even when both groups truly improved at the same rate.

Publish before/after 7/7 counts, paired wins and losses, baseline headroom and changes in each check for both groups. Keep all selected issuers in their original denominator. For every remaining non-pass, verify whether the reported reason agrees with the original filing or documented acquisition limit. Explain every regression, distinguishing a corrected old false pass from loss of a previously correct pass.

- **Pass the offline acceptance test** only if the numerical criterion, correctness samples and DOCN regression checks all pass on valid comparable inputs with the held-out group unexposed during development.
- **Fail** for a confirmed wrong new pass or the loss of a previously correct pass, regardless of the gains. With an adequately measurable cohort, failure to meet the numerical criterion is also a failure: report the observed gains and stop. If improvement was largely confined to development, report that it did not generalize. Do not tune toward held-out results.
- **Inconclusive** if baseline headroom makes the test impossible (fewer than five held-out companies were below 7/7 at baseline), required raw inputs are missing or not comparable, or independence was lost. Audit what is available, report any known defects, and do not claim a pass. A genuine, verified absence *in* a filing is a valid non-pass, not a missing test input.

### 3. Recompute newly passing checks, including an independent sample

A newly passing company is one that moved from fewer than seven passes to 7/7. Freeze the sampling method before seeing scores. Use recorded, reproducible draws without replacement, not selections based on the apparent ease or quality of a result.

Target sample: **at least eight distinct newly passing companies** for the first check (at least five held-out, at least one development, the rest from either group), and **at least three other** newly passing companies for the independent auditor, disjoint from the first. That needs at least eleven distinct new passes with that group mix.

**Whenever the normal 8+3 sample or its required group mix cannot be filled, check every new pass.** The first reviewer recomputes every newly passing company. The auditor independently recomputes a reproducibly drawn third of those same companies, rounded up, without seeing the first reviewer's conclusions. With six new passes, that means six checks and two independent rechecks; with one, both reviewers check it. Report the sample as exhaustive. A small number of new passes does not by itself make the test inconclusive. If **fewer than five held-out companies newly pass**, the numerical criterion has failed; apply the Fail rule (or the baseline-headroom Inconclusive rule if fewer than five could have improved), report the gains as they are, and still complete these checks so any wrong pass is reported. Never substitute an old pass, and never sample the same issuer twice within one check.

For each selected company, independently reconstruct every newly passing check from the original filing cells or sentences, not from extracted numbers. Cite the accession, locator, units, period and ownership or lease role as applicable. Verify the arithmetic, any negative evidence and disclosure coverage. Any confirmed wrong pass blocks acceptance.

### 4. Verify DOCN and confirm the live route separately

On the identical frozen DOCN inputs, compare canonical facts, check statuses, calculations and deterministic report sections to baseline; they must remain unchanged. In live confirmation, compare routing and evidence references separately from variable model prose.

**Only with separate paid-run approval:** run DOCN and two distinct held-out companies drawn from all 20, irrespective of their pass/fail status, using the same isolated setup as the pre-freeze MSFT run. The MSFT run counts as MSFT's live confirmation only if production code did not change between it and the frozen candidate; otherwise rerun MSFT on the frozen candidate. Predeclare a uniform draw without replacement, then draw once and record the seed and selection **after** the final offline score is frozen. Keep the Medium-depth runs within the approved total (proposed cap: **$55**); no automatic retries. If a required MSFT rerun would exceed the cap, stop and ask.

For each run, match the filing vintage and analysis cutoff to the offline result. Compare the seven source checks, the separate `material_claims` result and the graph route actually taken. Recompute the evidence behind a decision, or verify that a REVIEW reason is true. Confirm **at least one qualifying large filer actually reaches the debate and Portfolio Manager decision** before claiming the user-facing outcome. DOCN alone cannot establish it. Four correct REVIEWs demonstrate stopping behavior only. Report offline acceptance and live confirmation separately; while paid approval is pending, live confirmation is unverified.

### 5. Audit, gap closure and handoff

Independent audit: required

An independent audit by the existing Claude route is required on the frozen candidate. Supply the frozen requirements, source, score tables and sanitized receipts. The auditor verifies the independent sample and checks build commands and evidence for exposure to held-out filings, results or diagnostics outside authorized capture and scoring. Reproduce High findings locally before reporting them; close every in-scope Critical, High or Medium finding with regression evidence and re-audit the final change.

Preserve the verdict for the exact candidate that produced it. If a leak occurred, or an unsealed result prompted a correction, disclose it. The old cohort may verify a correction as regression evidence; it cannot independently validate the revised candidate. Do not tune and rescore those companies for a new generalization claim. Any fresh independent study is outside this plan.

In `docs/reviews/<date>-large-filer-coverage.md`, record the tested commits, frozen selection and inputs, both groups' results, verified reasons, manual and independent checks, DOCN comparison, live outcomes or pending status, audit findings and limitations.
